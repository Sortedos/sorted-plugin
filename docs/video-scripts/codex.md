# 60-second video: Sorted in Codex

Screens as checked on Windows 11 with Codex 0.155.1, 6 October 2026. Fake company: Acme Trading.

| Shot | On screen | Spoken |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Sorted dashboard of Acme Trading | Your business numbers live in Sorted. Let's put them inside Codex, so you can simply ask. |
| 2 | A terminal in the downloaded plugin folder | First, download this repository and open a terminal in its folder. |
| 3 | A PowerShell prompt asking "Paste your Sorted key", the key hidden | Save your personal Sorted key where Codex can read it. You paste it once, into your own computer, never into a chat. |
| 4 | `codex plugin marketplace add .` then `codex plugin add sorted@sorted` | Two commands add the Sorted plugin and its fourteen skills to Codex. |
| 5 | A new terminal: "Do a quick business review using Sorted." and the answer | Open a new terminal and ask for a business review. Codex answers from your live numbers, with the time of each snapshot. |
| 6 | "Send yesterday's Meta ads spend into Sorted" and a feed preview | For a system Sorted does not read, like Meta ads, Codex can send the numbers in. It shows you a preview first, and creates nothing until you say yes. |
| 7 | The new area on the dashboard, with its label | The numbers appear on your dashboard with a clear label. Your assistant read these numbers; Sorted cannot check them. |
